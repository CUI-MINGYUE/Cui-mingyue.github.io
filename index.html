Research

My research spans high-dimensional feature learning, sparse representation, and compressed sensing in signal processing; temporal data modeling and efficient neural architecture design in artificial intelligence; as well as intelligent processing and system integration, and structured representation and reconstruction of 3D scenes in computer vision and industrial software, with a focus on their interdisciplinary applications.
