I am now an Associate Researcher in School of Computer Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU). Before that I got my Ph.D. degree in School of Computer Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU).

Research

My research spans high-dimensional feature learning, sparse representation, and compressed sensing in signal processing; temporal data modeling and efficient neural architecture design in artificial intelligence; as well as intelligent sensing and system integration, and structured representation and reconstruction of 3D scenes in computer vision and industrial software, with a focus on their interdisciplinary applications.
